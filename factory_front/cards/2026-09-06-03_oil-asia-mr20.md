## The edge
After a large 20-hour move in oil, the next hour inside the Asian session gives part of it back. Horizon 1h; the tradeable form is one trade per day at 00:00 SAST against the prior 20h move, held to 09:00.

## Who pays
Hypothesis: the base-and-bounce anatomy SOLID monetises (over-extended NY sellers/buyers unwinding in the thin Asian book). The claim "already monetised by SOLID" was itself untested: it needed the correlation of this signature's daily P&L with SOLID's ledger.

## Cost term
C = 0.024/bbl round turn (spread 24 pts, no commission). Needs gross >= 0.072/bbl per fire.
